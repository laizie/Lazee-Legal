Lazee Support

Get in touch.

Lazee is built and supported by a single developer. If something's broken, confusing, or missing, email me directly.

Contact:

info@lazeeapp.com

What to include in your message

  • What you were trying to do
  • What actually happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About)
  • The Lazee version (Settings tab in the app, near the bottom)
  • Screenshots are great if the issue is visual

Response time

Most replies come within 1–3 days. Bug reports are prioritized. Feature requests are read but not always acted on immediately. Lazee aims to stay simple, so not every idea makes the cut.

Privacy

Lazee does not collect, see, or store your workout data. Read the full privacy policy for details.
